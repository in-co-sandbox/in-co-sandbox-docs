Welcome to the **Sandbox Help Centre**, your one-stop resource for quick answers, setup guidance, and troubleshooting help.

Whether you’re just starting with Sandbox APIs or looking for advanced integration guidance, the Help Centre gives you the context, structure, and resources all in one place.

### **What you’ll find here**

The Help Centre is organised into clear, topic-based categories so you can find exactly what you’re looking for:

- **Getting Started** – Learn about Sandbox APIs, authentication, environments, and setup.
- **Setting up your Workspace** – How to personalise your workspace, manage keys and access.
- **Billing & Payments** – Manage your account, subscriptions, billing queries, and invoices.
- **Developer Resources** – Step-by-step instructions, API reference, examples, integration best practices.
- **Legal Documents** – Terms of service, privacy policy, compliance & audit requirements.

Each article is designed to be concise, action-oriented, and easy to follow enabling you to get answers quickly.

### **How to use the Help Centre**

1. **Search for answers:** Use the search bar at the top to quickly find articles related to your query. Enter keywords like “authentication error” or “API key setup” to locate relevant guides instantly.
2. **Browse by category:** Explore the main sections — such as Getting Started or Developer Resources — to view articles grouped by topic. This helps you discover related content and understand the broader context.
3. **Follow article paths:** Each article may link to related guides, allowing you to move seamlessly from setup to troubleshooting without losing track of where you are.
4. **Contact support:** If you can’t find what you’re looking for, you can submit a ticket directly from the Help Centre interface for assistance.
