### **How to use the Help Centre**

1. **Search for answers:** Use the search bar at the top to quickly find articles related to your query. Enter keywords like “authentication error” or “API key setup” to locate relevant guides instantly.
2. **Browse by category:** Explore the main sections — such as Getting Started or Developer Resources — to view articles grouped by topic. This helps you discover related content and understand the broader context.
3. **Follow article paths:** Each article may link to related guides, allowing you to move seamlessly from setup to troubleshooting without losing track of where you are.
4. **Contact support:** If you can’t find what you’re looking for, you can submit a ticket directly from the Help Centre interface for assistance.
