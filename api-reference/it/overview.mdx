# Income Tax

Sandbox Income Tax APIs help automate profit & loss calculation and tax document extraction for investment transactions.

Income tax is a form of direct tax levied on the income generated by persons (Individuals, Companies, etc.). Capital gains from investment transactions are taxed based on holding period, asset class, and applicable tax regime.

### [Calculator APIs](https://developer.sandbox.co.in/api-reference/it/overview#calculator-api)

Sandbox's **Calculator APIs** compute profit & loss and capital gains tax impact for:

- **Domestic Securities**: Equity, mutual funds, and derivatives trading
- **Foreign Securities**: International investments with currency conversion
- **Cryptocurrency**: Digital asset transactions with holding period classification

### [Report APIs ](https://developer.sandbox.co.in/api-reference/it/overview#report-api)

**Report APIs** generate profit & loss reports for:

- **Securities Tax P&L**: Comprehensive P&L reporting for domestic and international securities
- **Crypto Tax P&L**: Gains/losses report for cryptocurrency transactions

### [OCR APIs](https://developer.sandbox.co.in/api-reference/it/overview#ocr-api)

Sandbox **OCR APIs** automate the extraction and classification of tax documents such as **Form 16**:

- Upload form PDFs and extract structured data
- Classify and parse relevant fields for tax workflows and verification

## Why Use Sandbox Income Tax APIs

Sandbox provides clean, easy-to-integrate APIs so you can:

- Calculate capital gains tax across multiple asset classes
- Extract data from tax documents automatically
- Generate compliance-ready tax P&L reports
- Support diverse investment portfolios
