---
title: 'Get e-Invoice by IRN'
description: "Fetch the e-Invoice by passing the invoice registration number (IRN). To fetch an e-Invoice, the authenticated taxpayer has to be a party in the transaction, either the seller or the buyer."
openapi: '/api-reference/gst/compliance/openapi.json GET /gst/compliance/tax-payer/e-invoice/{irn}'
---
<Info>
  **Authentication:** This endpoint does not use standard Sandbox JWT authentication. Pass the GST taxpayer `access_token` in the `Authorization` header. Refer to the [**Generate Taxpayer Session**](/recipes/gst/authentication/generate_tax_payer_session) recipe for steps to generate this token.
</Info>

<Info>
Only e-Invoices within the last 6 months can be fetched.
</Info>
