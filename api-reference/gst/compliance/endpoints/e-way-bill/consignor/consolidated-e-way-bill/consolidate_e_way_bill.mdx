## Who can generate a consolidated e-way bill?

<Info>
For each included e-way bill, the requester must be its generator or assigned transporter.
</Info>

Every included e-way bill must be active and within its validity period.

Source: [NIC API validations](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/version1.03/consolidated-eway-bill.html).
