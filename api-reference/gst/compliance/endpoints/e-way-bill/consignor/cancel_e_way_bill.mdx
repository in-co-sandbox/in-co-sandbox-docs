## Who can cancel an e-way bill?

<Info>
Only the generator of the e-way bill can cancel it, within 24 hours of generation. This includes a consignor, consignee, or transporter that generated the e-way bill. Being named in the e-way bill or assigned as its transporter is not sufficient.
</Info>

Source: [NIC API validations](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/version1.03/cancel-eway-bill.html) and [NIC e-way bill FAQ](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/html/faq.html).
