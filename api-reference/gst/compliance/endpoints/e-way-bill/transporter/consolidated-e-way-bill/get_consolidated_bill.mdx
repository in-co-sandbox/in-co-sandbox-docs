## Who can retrieve a consolidated e-way bill?

<Info>
The transporter must be the generator of the consolidated e-way bill. Being assigned to an individual e-way bill included in a consolidated e-way bill does not grant access.
</Info>

Source: [NIC API validations](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/version1.03/get-consolidated-eway-bill.html).
