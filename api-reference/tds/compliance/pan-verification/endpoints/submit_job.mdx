Save the [TRACES credentials](/api-reference/tds/compliance/traces-credentials/endpoints/save_credential) for the TAN in your Sandbox workspace. Sandbox manages TRACES authentication during processing.

After creating the job, upload the [JSON payee workbook](/api-reference/tds/compliance/pan-verification/payee_workbook) to `data.json_url` with HTTP PUT. Then use `data.job_id` to [check the job status](/api-reference/tds/compliance/pan-verification/endpoints/poll_job).

<Note>
Credential checks happen after the workbook upload. Missing or incorrect TRACES credentials appear as a `failed` job with a `message` in the status response.
</Note>

For cURL examples covering the complete workflow, see [Verify PANs in bulk](/recipes/tds/pan-verification/verify-pans-in-bulk).
