title: "About Sandbox"

---

Sandbox enables businesses to automate tax compliance using REST-like API. Our API has predictable resource-oriented URLs, accepts and returns JSON-encoded responses, and uses standard HTTP response codes, authentication, and verbs.

You can use the Sandbox API to build and automate End-to-End tax flows including

- TDS on Salary & Non-Salary Payments
- GST on Sales and Purchase Invoices
- Income Tax on Stock Investments & Crypto
- Tax KYC of Individuals and Businesses

If you don't have a Sandbox developer account, read more about it and sign up [<u>here</u>](https://accounts.sandbox.co.in/#/signup).
