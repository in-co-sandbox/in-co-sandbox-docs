---
title: "Invoices"
---

Sandbox generates different types of invoices for **Subscriptions, Wallet usage, and Overdrafts**. Each invoice type is designed to give you a clear view of your charges and payments. You can [view and download invoices](https://help.sandbox.co.in/portal/en/kb/articles/view-and-download-invoices) at any time.

### **Subscription Invoicing**

Subscriptions are prepaid, meaning customers pay at the start of each billing cycle (monthly or annually). Once paid, the subscription becomes active and includes access to 200+ APIs, API keys, and monthly quota. No additional subscription charges apply during the billing period unless you upgrade or change your plan.

Here's how to [Get a GST Invoice](https://help.sandbox.co.in/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-get-gst-invoice).

### **Wallet Invoicing**

The Sandbox Wallet follows a prepaid model. Customers add funds using a payment gateway or bank transfer, and these funds are used for APIs that incur wallet charges. Wallet charges are aggregated daily and deducted from your wallet balance. A Wallet Charges Invoice is generated summarizing all billable API usage.

### **Overdraft Invoicing**

Overdraft is postpaid and applies only when your monthly quota is exhausted. Additional API calls beyond quota are charged at the same per-call rate defined in your plan. At the end of each billing cycle, Sandbox raises a separate Overdraft Invoice listing all overdraft usage and charges.

Here's a guide on how to [Pay Invoices](https://help.sandbox.co.in/portal/en/kb/articles/pay-an-invoice).
