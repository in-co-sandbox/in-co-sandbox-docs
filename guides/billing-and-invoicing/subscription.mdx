---
title: "Subscription"
---

A subscription is a recurring payment arrangement where users are billed at regular intervals, called the billing period to access a product or service. To use Sandbox APIs, you must maintain an active subscription, billed monthly or annually through a valid payment method.

A Sandbox subscription gives you:

- Access to 200+ Sandbox APIs
- Access to API Keys
- A Monthly Quota, based on your selected plan

Here’s how you can [Purchase a subscription Plan](https://help.sandbox.co.in/portal/en/kb/articles/purchase-a-plan).

### **Free Trial**

Sandbox offers a [**14-day free trial**](https://accounts.sandbox.co.in/signup) that gives you full access to the platform so you can evaluate the APIs before committing to a paid plan. During the trial, you can:

- Test any API
- Build a proof of concept
- Share demos with your stakeholders

[**Here’s how to Start a Free Trial**](https://help.sandbox.co.in/portal/en/kb/articles/start-a-free-trial)
