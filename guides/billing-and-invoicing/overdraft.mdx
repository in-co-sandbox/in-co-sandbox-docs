We understand that at times your API calls may exceed the monthly quota. To avoid any production impact, you can opt for the Overdraft facility. This allows you to exceed your Monthly Quota. 

<Tip>
  **You can enable Overdraft from your Dashboard.**
</Tip>

However, for every call made after exceeding the quota, you will be charged an overdraft on a pro-rata basis as per your subscription plan.
