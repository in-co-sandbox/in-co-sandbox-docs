GST, which stands for Goods and Services Tax, is an indirect tax imposed on the supply of goods and services in India. It was implemented on July 1st, 2017. As GST follows a consumption-based tax model, it is applied at each point of sale.

Following categories of business are required to register under GST

| **Supply type** | **Turnover in a financial year** |
| :-------------: | :------------------------------: |
| Goods           | 40 Lakhs                         |
| Service         | 20 Lakhs                         |

Businesses are required to generate GST-compliant invoices, set off liability against available input credit, file GST returns, and reconcile sales and purchase ledgers. Non-compliance can lead to interest and penalties.
