## **Who Should Integrate with Sandbox?**

What makes Sandbox unique is our enriched APIs, which enables businesses to automate tax compliance workflows. In brief, if your business is scaling, Sandbox is going to be your one-stop-shop to solve these workflows:

### **KYC:**

Conduct a comprehensive [<u>KYC</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/kyc/), by fetching details related to PAN, TAN, GSTIN, Aadhaar, Bank, CIN/LLPIN, etc.

KYC APIs can help streamline onboarding for employees (HRMS), vendors (E-Commerce), borrowers (NBFCs), investors (brokerages) & more. Learn more about [<u>GST Public APIs</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/gst/), [<u>MCA Master Data APIs</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/kyc/), [<u>TAN Search APIs</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/tds/), etc.

### **Business:**

Use [<u>IT</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/income-tax/), [<u>GST</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/gst/) & [<u>TDS</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/tds/) Business APIs, build workflows to manage vendors, items/catalogues, payments, invoices to automate the necessary tax returns forms.

### **Compliance:**

Use [<u>IT</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/income-tax/), [<u>GST</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/gst/) & [<u>TDS</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/tds/) Compliance APIs to download, upload & e-file ITR, GSTR and TDS returns.

## **Why integrate with Sandbox?**

We set out to build a self-serve platform. Onboarding to Sandbox does not require any physical or offline intervention. This means all your tax compliance or verification processes can be automated to be done in real-time. Additionally, by making it completely automated, we remove any human error or downtime.

Importantly, Sandbox APIs are constantly updated by our team of CAs, on a day-to-day basis. We also ensure our products go through rigorous testing.

Additionally, we make sure that all the tax compliance requirements are met using simple plug-and-play APIs. Therefore, you can rely on Sandbox to handle your tax compliance requirements, while you focus on building your products & services.

## **How to integrate with Sandbox?**

Sandbox is a platform built by developers for developers. We, therefore, have designed the entire experience to suit the requirements of [<u>easy integration and deployment</u>](https://sandbox.co.in/guide/).

### **Documentation**

So, we made sure sandbox [<u>API documentation</u>](https://docs.sandbox.co.in/) is elaborative and comprehensive. The detailed requests and response structures allow developers to understand the APIs.

Additionally, our available SDK helps developers to integrate our APIs. Moreover, Sandbox APIs are RESTful APIs, making them an industry standard.

### **Free Trial**

Last but not least, our [<u>14-day free trial</u>](https://dashboard.sandbox.co.in/) allows developers to get their hands on and try the APIs on their own. This experience provides the developer and the business individuals with a clear understanding of the workings of Sandbox APIs. In conclusion, our APIs are designed to be simple and easy to integrate. The small integration efforts result in great gains by saving on time and money. It, therefore, makes sense.
